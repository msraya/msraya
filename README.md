### Biography
Manuel Sánchez Raya was born in Málaga, Spain, in 1971. He earned his degrees in Electronic Engineering and Computer Science from the University of Málaga, Spain, in 1992 and 1996, respectively. In 2013, he obtained his Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering from the University of Huelva, Huelva, Spain. 

Currently, he is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Automatic Systems Engineering at the University of Huelva. His research focuses on nuclear instrumentation, embedded systems, digital design, and real-time applications.
